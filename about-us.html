Our Story

Established in 1932, Vintage Restaurant & Bar stands as a timeless symbol of classic elegance and culinary excellence. What began as a cozy family bistro has grown into a cherished landmark, where history, flavor, and hospitality come together.

For over a century, we’ve perfected the art of blending traditional recipes with modern finesse. From our wood-fired grills to handcrafted cocktails, every dish and drink carries the legacy of generations dedicated to the craft of fine dining.

Whether it’s an intimate dinner, a grand celebration, or a quiet evening at the bar, Vintage offers a warm, nostalgic ambiance that invites you to savor every moment — just as guests have done for more than 100 years.
