Shipping Policy

Read about our shipping process and delivery details at Vintage Restaurant & Bar.

At Vintage Restaurant & Bar – Since 1932, we strive to ensure that your orders are delivered safely and promptly. This Shipping Policy outlines how we handle deliveries, shipping times, and related procedures.

1. Order Processing

Once an order is placed, our team processes it within 24 hours. You will receive a confirmation email or message with your order details and estimated delivery time.

2. Shipping Methods & Delivery Time

  • Standard Delivery: 3–5 business days within the city limits.
  • Express Delivery: 1–2 business days for urgent orders (extra charges may apply).
  • Delivery times may vary during holidays or peak seasons.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping fees are calculated based on the order value, location, and delivery method. Free standard shipping may be available on selected promotions or for orders above a certain amount.

4. Order Tracking

You can track your order using the tracking link sent to your email or by contacting our support team. Please keep your order number handy for faster assistance.

5. Delivery Issues

If there are delays, missing items, or damages during shipping, please contact our support team immediately. We will work to resolve the issue promptly.

6. International Shipping

Currently, we only deliver within India. International shipping is not available at this time.
