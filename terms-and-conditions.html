Rules & Regulations

To ensure a safe, enjoyable, and respectful dining experience for all our guests at Vintage Restaurant & Bar, we request you to follow these simple guidelines.

General Conduct
  1. Guests are expected to maintain respectful behavior towards staff and other patrons at all times.
  2. Any form of harassment, misconduct, or disturbance will not be tolerated.
  3. Children must be accompanied and supervised by adults at all times.
Dining & Reservation Policies
  1. Reservations are confirmed only upon receiving a confirmation message or email from our team.
  2. Tables will be held for a maximum of 15 minutes past your reserved time.
  3. Outside food and beverages are strictly prohibited within the premises.
  4. We reserve the right to deny entry or service based on inappropriate dress or behavior.
Payments & Cancellations
  1. We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, and digital wallets.
  2. Cancellations must be made at least 2 hours prior to the reservation time.
  3. Any damages caused to property or furnishings will be chargeable.
Bar & Alcohol Policy
  1. Alcohol is served only to guests aged 21 and above with valid ID proof.
  2. Management reserves the right to refuse service to intoxicated guests.
  3. Outside liquor is not permitted under any circumstances.
Takeaway & Home Delivery Policies
  1. All takeaway and delivery orders must be placed through our official website, app, or contact number.
  2. Orders once confirmed cannot be modified or canceled after preparation has begun.
  3. Delivery timings may vary based on location, weather, and order volume.
  4. Customers are requested to check their orders upon delivery; complaints after 30 minutes of delivery will not be entertained.
  5. All takeaway and delivery packaging is chargeable and included in the final bill.
  6. Our delivery personnel will deliver orders only to the address confirmed during order placement.
  7. For safety reasons, delivery agents are not permitted to enter customer premises.
  8. In case of online payment failures, please contact our support team for assistance before re-ordering.
Safety & Security
  1. For everyone’s safety, CCTV cameras are installed across the premises.
  2. Smoking is permitted only in designated areas.
  3. Guests are responsible for their personal belongings; the management will not be liable for any loss.

Note: By entering Vintage Restaurant & Bar or placing an order (dine-in, takeaway, or delivery), guests agree to abide by these rules. The management reserves the right to modify these terms at any time.
