Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us at Vintage Restaurant & Bar.

At Vintage Restaurant & Bar – Since 1932, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you interact with our website or services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal details when you make a reservation, subscribe to our offers, or contact us through our website. This may include:

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number)
  • Reservation details (date, time, guest count)
  • Payment and billing information (when applicable)
  • Feedback and communication history

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us improve your dining experience. We use the data to:

  • Confirm and manage table reservations
  • Send order updates, offers, and promotions
  • Respond to queries and provide support
  • Enhance our website experience and improve services

3. Data Protection

We maintain strict security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Sensitive information is encrypted and stored securely.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or trade your personal data. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers (such as delivery partners or payment processors) to fulfill your requests efficiently.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance browsing experience, remember your preferences, and analyze site performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings if desired.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information at any time. Please contact us if you wish to make such a request.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The updated version will always be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
